Good to know

Why some treatments say “Price on booking”

Why isn't every price listed here?

Some treatments depend on you — your nail length and art detail, your skin type and history, or the waxing areas combined. Rather than publish a price that doesn't fit your appointment, we confirm the exact price on WhatsApp before you book. What you see is what you pay.

Intricate nail art & designs

Every set is bespoke. Whether you want chrome powder, French tips, foil or hand-painted art, send us a screenshot on WhatsApp and we will confirm the price and the time needed before your slot.

Personal skin diagnostics

Lamelle and clinical treatments depend on your skin type, hydration and previous treatments. Message us first and we will talk through what suits you — and whether a consultation before treatment is the right step.

How do I book?

Everything runs on WhatsApp: message 079 663 2367 with the treatment, your preferred date and a morning or afternoon slot. Prefer a calendar? The Fresha portal is linked on the Booking page.
