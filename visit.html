Frequently asked visit questions

Finding us & preparing for your session

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes — all visits are by confirmed appointment, so please message us on WhatsApp at 079 663 2367 before arriving. That keeps every visit relaxed and one-on-one, with no waiting.

Where exactly do I park?

We offer easy on-site parking right outside our entrance at 73 Kenneth Kaunda Road. If you are unsure on the day, message us on WhatsApp and we will guide you in.

Can I bring style inspiration photos?

Absolutely. Take screenshots of nail art, lash styles or brow shaping you adore and send them on WhatsApp before your visit — we will confirm the price and any prep needed.

What if I need to reschedule?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can so we can move your slot and offer the time to another client.
