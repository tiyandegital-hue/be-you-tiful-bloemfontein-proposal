Frequently asked booking questions

Before you message us

How do I book an appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp at 079 663 2367 with the treatment you want, your preferred date and a morning or afternoon slot. Use the templates above — they write the message for you. You can also book through our Fresha portal.

When is the studio open?

Monday to Thursday 08:00–18:00, Friday 08:00–17:00, Saturday 08:00–13:00. Sundays and public holidays are closed. Visits are by appointment, so please book before arriving.

Can I book appointments for two people at the same time?

Mention it in your WhatsApp message — for example a duo booking with a friend or family member — and we will confirm what is possible for your chosen date.

How do I know the exact price before I book?

Published specials are listed on the Specials page with their validity dates. For anything else, send us a WhatsApp message and we will confirm the exact price before your slot is booked — nothing is charged that you didn't agree to first.

What if I need to reschedule?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can so we can move your slot and offer the time to another client. The sooner you tell us, the easier it is to find you a new time.
