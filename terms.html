Bookings

All visits are by confirmed appointment. A booking is only confirmed once the studio replies on WhatsApp (or through Fresha) confirming your date and time. Sending a message is a request, not a confirmed booking.

Prices & specials

Prices shown on this site are the studio's published prices. Specials apply only within the validity dates shown on the Specials page; once a special's window has passed it no longer applies. For treatments without a published price, the exact price is confirmed on WhatsApp before your booking is confirmed — nothing is charged that you did not agree to first.

Rescheduling

If you need to move your appointment, message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can so we can offer your slot to another client and find you a new time.

Gallery images

Gallery images on this site are sample illustrations of the styles the studio creates, clearly labelled as such, until the studio supplies real photos of its own work. They are not photographs of real treatments.
